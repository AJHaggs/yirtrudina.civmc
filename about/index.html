About Republica Yirtrudina

Be Important, Make a Difference

Republica Yirtrudina is a fictional country in the Minecraft server known as CivMC.

Also known as Yirtrudis, Yirtrudina is a socialist republic in the (+,-) quadrant that thrives on tourism and trade.

We value social equality, community, and scalable development.

How to join

To join Republica Yirtrudina, you must be a member of the CivMC Minecraft and Discord servers.

Once you've joined both, join the Discord server right here and fill out a citizenship request form.
